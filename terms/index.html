No Black Boxes offers three course tracks: Build a Brain (ages 15–18), the No Black Boxes Intensive (ages 18+), and the Experimental Neuroscience Bootcamp (researchers and PhD students).

Applications and selection

Places on guided courses are limited and subject to an application process. Submission of an application does not guarantee a place. We select based on motivation and suitability, not prior technical experience.

Course fees

Where a course fee applies, it will be stated clearly at the time of application. Payment is required to secure your place.

Cancellations by participants

  • Cancellations made more than 30 days before the course start date: full refund
  • Cancellations made 14–30 days before the course start date: 50% refund
  • Cancellations made fewer than 14 days before the course start date: no refund

Cancellations by No Black Boxes

In the unlikely event that we must cancel a course, we will notify all registered participants as soon as possible and offer a full refund or a place on a future course.

Online courses are delivered via live video sessions and recorded lectures. Recordings may be made available to participants for a limited period after the course. By participating, you consent to being present in a recorded session.
